Account Deletion

If you have created an account within Noor-e-Istikhara, you have the right to request full deletion of your account and its associated personal data.

How to delete your account:
  1. Open the Noor-e-Istikhara mobile application.
  2. Navigate to the Settings tab.
  3. Tap on Account Management and scroll to the bottom.
  4. Select Delete Account and confirm the prompt.

Note: Deleting your account is permanent. Your query history and preferences will be erased from our servers immediately and cannot be recovered.
